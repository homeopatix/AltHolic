AltHolic 4.90 - Launcher Shift+Click Fix

- Restores reliable Shift+Left Click on the floating AltHolic launcher to toggle Settings.
- Uses Turbine.UI.Control.IsShiftKeyDown() / IsAltKeyDown(), the documented static modifier-key API.
- Removes the old account-name prerequisite from Shift+Click; Settings is always available.
- Uses actual window visibility instead of only the saved visibility flag when toggling Settings.
- Preserves the single-launcher architecture, drag behavior, tooltip, and 4.74 anti-tiling behavior.
